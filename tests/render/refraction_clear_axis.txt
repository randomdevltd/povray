optical-only zero-coefficient mix-replace medium; this must not replace the
independent index field. All screen coordinates must follow the independent
Fermat/Snell formula in refraction_prepared_ray_reference.awk within .001 scene
unit. One interior and one slab boundary pair avoid coincident-container effects.

AAoff and two threads. No rendered reference or power adjustment is used.

Clear2 adds a separate overlapping interior with refraction off. It must not
clear the other interior's index contribution; the same Fermat ray remains.
