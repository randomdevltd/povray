Joint camera/source integration measurement contract

The scene separates the emitting field from its scattering receiver. The source
is a colored uniform box of fixed generated flux100lm. The receiver is a narrow
unit-depth slab with RGB absorption and scattering. Case0 is homogeneous;
Case1 has two constant-density voxels in one container; Case2 adds an overlapping multiply
modifier; Case3 adds an asymmetric opaque plate. Case4 preserves the original adjacent-container diagnostic outside the accepting
runner until its existing boundary discrepancy is resolved. Every accepting
slab has an explicit density field to activate prepared fixed-method4.
Phase0 is normalized isotropic;
Phase±0.4 selects normalized Henyey–Greenstein scattering.

The reference integrates camera depth and the actual three-dimensional source
volume by independent Cartesian quadrature. Every source connection and camera
segment uses exact axis-box intersections through the piecewise-constant optical
field. RGB extinction includes scattering. There is no surface cosine, source
bounding-sphere substitution, or normalization to a rendered image. Source and
camera quadrature must converge within0.5% before comparisons are accepted.
The reference phase integral is independently checked to unity.

First compare immutable old and collector-evaluate-all builds with identical
scene/argv/seed and decoded RGB pixels. Require exact equality for the stride1
fixed-quadrature path. Record separately any legacy stride>1 path retained by
the collector; do not claim exactness for a path whose quadrature changed.

Then compare the joint-budget build against the independent integral. Start
with8x8 linear16bitRGB frames, AAoff, explicit sourceSamples256. For these new
single-scattering fixtures predeclare channel mean relative error<=3%, with a
floor of two16bit quantum steps; compare actual pixel coordinates when computing
image means. Report pixel RMS separately, without conflating smoothness and
correct mean. Repeat homogeneous and voxel fixtures at resolution0.05/0.025:
require both means pass the independent reference and their difference<=3%.
This tests finite optical-field quadrature convergence honestly; it does not
assert arbitrary coarse grids encode an identical heterogeneous field.
Use fixed scene/seed settings across immutable builds. No per-cell minimum
source draw is allowed in the joint estimator. SourceSamples is a budget for a
geometric TraceRay leg, including its numerical curved chords; recursive paths
start separate budgets. Atmosphere and containing-media calls share the leg
budget while retaining their existing ordered composition; their physical
coefficients are not merged by this sampling change.

These focused tests do not establish the cost of integrated scenes containing
variable scattering, refractive media, geometry and several light sources.
Such scenes require matched camera framing, no anti-aliasing, and measurements
of total instructions, preparation, media samples, shadow tests and bending.
Full-frame output is needed to assess overall lighting, noise and shadow detail.
